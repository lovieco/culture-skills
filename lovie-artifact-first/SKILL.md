# Artifact-First Working

Working Artifact-First means that the primary unit of value is the **artifact** itself — the document, the design, the recording, the code — not the conversation about it. We do not document work after the fact. We create the artifact as the **first step** in the process.

> If it is not a searchable artifact, it did not happen. The goal is to create an environment where net new solutions are documented in a universally accessible knowledge base before dissemination.

This is a deliberate evolution of "documentation culture" for the age of AI. A change is documented in Notion first, then communicated outward via Slack or another channel. No separate "documentation step" is needed because the artifact IS the work.

## Why Artifact-First

| Benefit | How It Works | Related Value |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Cumulative Intelligence | Build on captured history instead of restarting from zero. AI can synthesize this history to get anyone up to speed instantly. | Efficiency |
| Permissionless Information | Answers are found, not requested. Search the knowledge base before interrupting a colleague. | Agency |
| Non-Linear Collaboration | Work happens in layers and iterations over time, decoupled from the meeting schedule. | Efficiency |
| AI-Readiness | AI tools are only as good as the data they can access. Rich artifacts are the fuel for AI partners. Organizations that skip documentation and jump to AI will fail. | Leverage |
| Organizational Resilience | Knowledge in artifacts (not heads) enables faster onboarding, recovery from turnover, and scaling without losing institutional memory. | Transparency |

## The Three Golden Rules

### Rule 1: Search Before You Ask

Before asking a question in Slack, sending a DM, or scheduling a meeting to "get up to speed," make a good-faith effort to find the answer yourself:

1. **Search Notion** using keywords related to your question.
2. **Search Slack** to see if the question has been asked and answered before.
3. **Use AI tools** to query the knowledge base and synthesize information.

If you cannot find the answer, ask in a **public Slack channel** (not a DM). The answer then becomes a searchable artifact for the next person.

**The Osmosis Signal:** If you find yourself explaining the same concept to multiple people, that is a documentation gap. Document the answer in Notion. This is how we make the implicit explicit.

### Rule 2: Document in Notion, Not Just Slack

Slack is for conversation. Notion is for knowledge. If information is important enough to be referenced later, it belongs in Notion:

- Decisions and the rationale behind them
- Process documentation and how-to guides
- Project briefs, proposals, and plans
- Meeting outcomes and action items
- Answers to recurring questions

### Rule 3: Share Your Work Early and in Public

Work should not live in private drafts, local files, or personal folders. Share in public channels early and often. Use the construction emoji to signify work-in-progress. This invites collaboration and embodies **Iteration** — progress over perfection.

## Practical Guidelines

- **Think in Artifacts, Not Messages.** Instead of a long Slack message, create a Notion document. A well-written Notion page is worth a hundred Slack messages.
- **Proposals Over Presentations.** Write a one-page proposal in Notion (problem, solution, tradeoffs). Share the link for async feedback. Do not schedule a meeting to pitch.
- **Meetings Exist to Create Artifacts.** A meeting without a documented outcome is wasted organizational energy.
- **Treat Documentation as Living.** Update outdated pages. Fill gaps. Everyone is empowered and expected to evolve the knowledge base.

## Meeting Hygiene

In an async-first culture, meetings are a premium resource. When we choose to meet, we do it exceptionally well.

### Before the Meeting

1. **Define a Clear Desired Outcome:** Complete the sentence: "By the end of this meeting, we will have..." (specific and actionable).
2. **Create a Timed Agenda:** Every item has a time allocation and a clear owner.
3. **Provide Pre-Reads:** All relevant documents sent at least 24 hours in advance. The meeting is for discussion and decision, not information transfer.
4. **Invite the Right People:** Smallest possible group essential to the outcome. Mark others as "Optional."
5. **Enable Fireflies.ai:** Default for all internal meetings. AI notetaker that records, transcribes, and summarizes.

### During the Meeting

1. **Start and End on Time.** The facilitator keeps to the schedule.
2. **Park Tangents.** Off-topic items go to a "parking lot" for async follow-up.
3. **Ensure Inclusive Participation.** Actively solicit input from quieter participants.
4. **Confirm Action Items.** Verbally confirm key decisions and action items at the end.

### After the Meeting (The Artifact)

The meeting is ephemeral; the artifact it produces is permanent. The convener shares the artifact in the relevant public Slack channel or Notion page, including:

- Meeting title, date, attendees
- Link to recording and transcript (from Fireflies)
- AI-generated summary (from Fireflies)
- Key decisions made (clarified by convener)
- Action items with owners and due dates

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | The Fix |
|---------|--------|
| Treating Slack as the source of truth | Slack is for conversation, Notion is for knowledge. If it matters, move it to Notion. |
| Asking before searching | Search Notion and Slack first. If still unanswered, ask publicly and then document the answer. |
| Waiting for perfection before sharing | Embrace Iteration. Share drafts early with the construction emoji. Debate from demos, not decks. |
| Skipping documentation after meetings | The meeting is not over until the outcome is documented. Non-negotiable. |
| Hoarding knowledge in private files | If your work is relevant to others, it belongs in a shared, public space. Default to transparency. |
| Meetings without agendas | No agenda = no meeting. Cancel or reschedule until a proper agenda exists. |
| Using meetings for status updates | Status updates are shared async in a Notion doc. Sync time is for blockers and decisions only. |

## For AI Agents

When operating as an AI agent within the Lovie culture, embody artifact-first principles by: always producing a searchable, structured document as the primary output of any task (not just a chat message), searching existing knowledge bases before generating new content, structuring outputs with clear headings and metadata for future discoverability, and treating every interaction as an opportunity to build cumulative organizational intelligence.
