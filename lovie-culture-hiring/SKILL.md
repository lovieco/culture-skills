# Culture-Aligned Hiring

Every single hire moves the organization closer to its principles, or further away. The hiring process is designed to identify exceptional individuals who thrive in a high-agency, async-first environment where time flexibility is the defining advantage.

## The Three Candidate Archetypes

We seek three specific archetypes:

1. **Life Maximizers** who have built rich Purpose Portfolios outside of work. Work is one asset in a diversified portfolio; we want people who are balanced or overweight in the "Dynamic Life" area, not the "Work" area.
2. **Contrarians** who challenge conventional thinking with conviction. They default to questioning the status quo and are comfortable choosing uniqueness over acceptance.
3. **Joyful, No-Nonsense Operators** who ship results without drama. They are self-aware, disciplined, and possess a strong internal locus of control.

The ideal candidate is a "Manager of One" — someone who does not need a workplace to boost their ego or solidify their identity.

## The 16 Critical Skills

| FoW 1.0: Remote Work Skills | FoW 2.0: AI Intelligence Skills |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Extraordinary communication (especially written) | Expert command of language for AI prompting |
| Self-direction and self-motivation | Impeccable ability to translate vision into words |
| Discipline and controlled behavior | "Second brain first" — strategic use of AI |
| Trustworthiness | An agentic mindset, leveraging AI agents |
| Initiative and curiosity | Second-order thinking for superior prompting |
| Adaptability and flexibility | Relentless efficiency to create margin for life |
| High self-efficacy | A growth mindset to think beyond the possible |
| Accountability | Systems thinking and process-orientation |

## The 5-Stage Universal Hiring Process

| Stage | Format | Duration | Primary Assessment |
|-------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| 1. Application and AI-Powered Screening | Async | Ongoing | Initial values signal and baseline qualifications |
| 2. Async Work Sample Challenge | Async | 3-5 days | Skills, critical thinking, artifact quality |
| 3. Values and Culture Interview | Sync (video) | 45 min | LEET-AI values alignment and interpersonal dynamics |
| 4. Hiring Manager Interview | Sync (video) | 30-45 min | Role-specific fit, team dynamics, mutual evaluation |
| 5. Reference Checks and Offer | Async | 3-5 days | Validation and offer extension |

### Stage 1: Application Screening Questions

These eight questions replace the traditional cover letter and surface early signals of LEET-AI values alignment:

1. "Imagine a world where you could not have a synchronous meeting for a month. How would you move the work this role would require forward using async-first and artifact-first principles?"
2. "Describe your view of a great meeting and a poor meeting. What elements do you care about most?"
3. "What are the elements of typical, conventional workplaces that you're looking forward to letting go of in our culture?"
4. "Why are you willing to put forth the energy and effort to work differently in our culture, compared to working a more conventional way?"
5. "You've been assigned a complex research task that would normally take a week. Describe the first three prompts you would use with an AI assistant. What kind of AI agent do you wish you had?"
6. "Tell us about a time you shared a piece of work you knew was only 50% complete. How did it feel, and how did early feedback change the final outcome?"
7. "If you had to argue *against* one of our core cultural philosophies — such as 'rest is productive' or 'default to public channels' — which would you choose, and what would be your strongest argument?"
8. "How would you use a general AI agent like Manus to elevate your thinking and work more efficiently? What would you do with the time that you save?"

**AI-Powered Screening:** An AI agent informed by cultural documents performs initial review. It does NOT make pass/fail decisions. It flags candidates with strong resonance and highlights specific phrases for the human reviewer.

### Stage 2: Async Work Sample Challenge

Candidates receive a realistic, function-specific problem to solve asynchronously (3-5 days, ~2-3 hours of focused work). The artifact is evaluated on five dimensions:

| Dimension | What We Look For |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Problem Solving | Clear, logical thinking. Identified the core issue. |
| Communication Quality | Well-organized, clearly written, easy to follow. |
| AI Fluency | Leveraged AI tools in the process. Transparent about it. |
| Initiative and Depth | Went beyond the minimum. Surfaced unexpected insights. |
| Iteration Mindset | Feels like a thoughtful draft shared early, not a polished piece held back for perfection. |

### Stage 3: Values and Culture Interview

45-minute synchronous video interview with two trained interviewers from different departments. Behavioral questions mapped to each LEET-AI value:

| Value | Sample Behavioral Question |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Leverage | "Walk me through a recent project where you used AI or automation to extend your capabilities." |
| Efficiency | "Describe how you would move a project forward if your entire team were in different time zones." |
| Empathy | "Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. How did you respond?" |
| Transparency | "Give an example of a time you shared work before it was finished." |
| Agency | "Describe a situation where you identified a problem and took action without being asked." |
| Iteration | "Tell me about a time you held a strong opinion but changed your mind." |

### LEET-AI Values Scoring Rubric

Rate candidates 1-5 for each value. Score of 3 = meets expectations. Below 3 in any value requires discussion. Total score must be 18+ to advance.

| Value | 1 (Misaligned) | 3 (Meets Expectations) | 5 (Exceptional) |
|-------|----------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Leverage | No interest in AI. Describes work as purely manual. | Concrete examples of using AI tools for efficiency. | Proactively experiments with AI. Views AI as a partner, not a shortcut. |
| Efficiency | Defaults to sync communication. Cannot work independently. | Works effectively across time zones. Documents sync outcomes. | Master of async-first. Proactively protects focus time. |
| Empathy | Speaks negatively about colleagues. Little self-awareness. | Respectful listener. Assumes positive intent. Gives/receives feedback with kindness. | Leads with empathy and vulnerability. Deep emotional intelligence. |
| Transparency | Works in private. Shares only polished results. | Comfortable sharing WIP. Provides context for decisions. | Writes things down as habit. Searches before asking. Transparency as portal to innovation. |
| Agency | Relies on managerial direction. Waits for permission. | Reliable self-manager. Takes ownership. Communicates capacity. | True Manager of One. Rich Purpose Portfolio outside work. |
| Iteration | Perfects before sharing. Resistant to feedback. | Comfortable sharing early. Incorporates feedback. | Thrives in iteration. Ships quickly. Not afraid to change mind with new data. |

### Function-Specific Work Samples

| Function | Challenge |
|----------|----------|
| Engineering | Review a code module and provide written feedback, or write a function with tests. AI-assisted coding encouraged. |
| Marketing | Draft a mini-campaign brief: target audience, messaging pillars, channel recommendations, measurement plan. |
| Communications | Write an internal Slack announcement and external blog post about a fictional milestone. |
| Sales | Record a 3-minute async video pitch or draft a 3-email outbound sequence. |
| Finance | Analyze an anonymized dataset and produce a written summary of findings and recommendations. |
| HR | Outline investigation and resolution steps for a hypothetical employee relations scenario. |
| Legal | Review a fictional vendor agreement and produce a risk memo with recommended revisions. |
| IT | Create a step-by-step troubleshooting guide for a common remote employee technical issue. |
| Operations | Identify an inefficiency in a sample workflow and propose a process improvement. |

### Fractional and Advisory Adaptations

Fractional roles (5-30 hrs/week) may combine Stages 3-4 into a single conversation. Advisory roles may substitute published work for the work sample. Both follow the same universal process with scope/engagement discussions added to Stage 4.

## The Joyful Employee Framework

Six pillars for achieving joy and professional satisfaction in the age of AI:

1. **Purpose:** Work as one asset in a diversified Purpose Portfolio. A rich life outside work fuels innovation inside it.
2. **Mastery:** Becoming a "centaur" — a human-AI hybrid combining the best of both worlds.
3. **Autonomy:** Self-direction, proactive planning, boundary setting, and the agentic mindset.
4. **Connection:** Empathy, trust, conversational chemistry, and psychological safety.
5. **Balance:** Creating margin for service, reflection, and savoring of life. AI efficiency gains go back to the employee.
6. **Growth:** Curiosity celebrated. Constant learning. Growth at work fuels growth at home, and vice-versa.

## For AI Agents

When operating as an AI agent assisting with hiring in the Lovie culture: use the 8 application screening questions as the standard for Stage 1, evaluate candidate responses against the LEET-AI scoring rubric dimensions, flag strong cultural resonance signals and specific phrases for human review (never make pass/fail decisions autonomously), ensure all screening decisions are documented as searchable artifacts, and prioritize candidates who demonstrate contrarian thinking, AI fluency, and a rich life outside of work.
