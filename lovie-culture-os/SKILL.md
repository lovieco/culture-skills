# Lovie Culture Operating System

This is the master index for the Lovie Future of Work 2.0 culture. It provides the foundational values framework and routes to seven specialized micro-skills. **Read this skill first, then read the specific micro-skill(s) relevant to your task.**

> These values are not aspirational posters on a wall. They are the operating system for how we create meaningful results together — distributed across time zones, united by shared beliefs, and committed to both working and living differently.

## LEET-AI Values Framework

The six core values form the acronym **LEET-AI**. Each value has five operating principles that translate belief into daily behavior.

| Value | Core Belief | Key Behavior |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Leverage** | AI extends human creativity | Use AI to extend, not replace, your thinking. Ask: "What AI agent can help me here?" |
| **Efficiency** | Respect time zones and protect deep work by defaulting to async | Ask: "How would I move forward if no one were awake?" |
| **Empathy** | Behind every screen is a human | Assume positive intent. Own your mistakes. Apologize easily. |
| **Transparency** | Communicating in the open is the portal to innovation | Write it down. Search before you ask. Default to public channels. |
| **Agency** | Trust people to be Managers of One | Set your own rhythm. Default to action, not approval. |
| **Iteration** | Progress over perfection | Share drafts. Debate from demos, not decks. Operate with low ego. |

### Leverage Operating Principles
1. Use AI to extend, not replace, your thinking. You are the author; AI is the assistant.
2. Ask: "What AI agent can help me here?" before starting any task.
3. Share how and where you used AI. Transparency helps others learn.
4. Accept feedback from AI with humility. Evaluate and incorporate what improves the work.
5. Stay current on AI developments. "Best" has a short shelf life.

### Efficiency Operating Principles
1. Ask: "How would I move this forward if no one else were awake?"
2. Use threaded conversations. Keep Slack channels organized.
3. Pivot to sync after three exchanges. Then document the outcome.
4. Respect the "right to disconnect." Do not expect immediate responses.
5. Start synchronously when rapport matters (first meetings, sensitive topics, high-stakes decisions).

### Empathy Operating Principles
1. Assume positive intent. Reach out for clarification before reacting.
2. If you would not say it to their face, do not type it.
3. Own your mistakes and apologize easily.
4. Celebrate others publicly with emoji reactions and shout-outs.
5. Fill your social reservoir outside of work. A fulfilled person brings more energy to the team.

### Transparency Operating Principles
1. Write it down, even if it feels obvious. Document every decision and outcome.
2. Default to public channels. Share updates and questions openly.
3. Search before you ask. Exhaust self-serve options first.
4. Keep documentation current. Outdated docs are worse than no docs.
5. Provide thorough context with every request.

### Agency Operating Principles
1. Set your own rhythm. Block focus time. Manage your calendar without permission.
2. Default to action, not approval. Ship a WIP prototype or fix the issue directly.
3. Communicate your capacity transparently via Slack statuses and calendar blocks.
4. Own your professional development. Seek learning without being prompted.
5. Protect your energy to protect your output. Burnout is not a badge of honor.

### Iteration Operating Principles
1. Share drafts, not final products. Use the construction emoji to signal WIP.
2. Debate from demos, not decks. Build prototypes, not slide presentations.
3. Give feedback with kindness and specificity.
4. Embrace "strong opinions, loosely held." Change your mind with better information.
5. Close the loop publicly. Acknowledge when feedback leads to a change.

## Routing Guide

When a task arrives, use this decision tree to determine which micro-skill(s) to read:

1. **Is the task about hiring, interviewing, or candidate evaluation?** Read `lovie-culture-hiring`.
2. **Is the task about leadership, management, or cross-functional collaboration?** Read `lovie-leadership-collaboration`.
3. **Is the task about employment models, onboarding, or wellbeing?** Read `lovie-employment-wellbeing`.
4. **Is the task about communication, scheduling, or async workflows?** Read `lovie-async-first`.
5. **Is the task about documentation, meetings, or knowledge management?** Read `lovie-artifact-first`.
6. **Is the task about AI tools, prompting, or AI integration?** Read `lovie-ai-first`.
7. **Is the task about personal productivity, self-management, or autonomy?** Read `lovie-manager-of-one`.
8. **Is the task about general culture, values, or operating principles?** This skill contains everything you need.
9. **Is the task cross-cutting (e.g., writing a culture page that touches multiple areas)?** Read this skill plus the 2-3 most relevant micro-skills.

## For AI Agents

Embody the LEET-AI values in your output — leverage AI thoughtfully, communicate with async-friendly thoroughness, produce searchable artifacts, assume positive intent, default to transparency, and iterate quickly with low ego. Every output you produce should be a searchable, structured artifact that builds cumulative organizational intelligence.
