# Manager of One

A Manager of One is a self-directed, proactive individual who takes full responsibility for their own productivity and outcomes. They do not wait to be told what to do. They manage their own time, prioritize their tasks, and communicate their progress without constant oversight. They are the CEOs of their own roles.

This concept, originally articulated by the founders of Basecamp, is the bedrock of the Lovie culture values of **Agency** and **Efficiency**.

## Why It Matters

| Benefit | How It Works | Related Value |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Unlocks True Agency | Self-management gives freedom to design your workday around your life. Foundation of the non-linear workday. | Agency |
| Drives Peak Efficiency | Masters of focus who eliminate distractions, protect deep work time, and produce higher-quality output in less time. | Efficiency |
| Builds Radical Trust | Proactive communication and reliable delivery build accountability. Trust is the currency of a distributed team. | Transparency |
| Reduces Overhead | Traditional management shifts from task-tracking to coaching, mentorship, and removing blockers. | Iteration |

## Core Systems for Self-Management

### 1. Notion: Single Source of Truth for Tasks

All significant tasks live in a personal or team-based task board in Notion (Kanban: To Do, In Progress, Done). Create a dedicated "Inbox" list. Any time a new task or idea surfaces, capture it immediately without triaging in the moment.

**Daily Triage (15 min):** For each inbox item, apply the 4D framework: **Do** (under 2 min), **Defer** (move to To Do with priority), **Delegate** (assign to someone else), or **Delete** (no longer relevant).

### 2. Slack: Triage and Communication Hub

Slack is for discussion, not task storage. Triage messages into actions or artifacts.

- Use **"Save for Later"** for items needing thoughtful response.
- Use **"Remind Me"** for follow-ups at a specific time.
- Move real tasks out of Slack into Notion with a link back for context.

### 3. Email: External Input Channel

Check at specific times, process to zero using the same 4D framework. Forward task-bearing emails to Notion.

### 4. Calendar: The Time-Boxing Tool

Block non-negotiable deep work time at the start of each week. Schedule specific short blocks for input processing (e.g., "Email & Slack Triage" at 10 AM and 4 PM). Keep Slack and email closed outside these blocks. Leave buffer time; a perfectly packed calendar is a fragile calendar.

## Habit Stacking

Link new Manager of One habits to existing routines using the formula: **After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT].**

Examples:
- After morning coffee, triage Notion inbox for 15 minutes.
- After closing a Slack conversation with an action item, add it to Notion inbox immediately.
- After finishing a meeting, document and share action items immediately.
- Before closing laptop for the day, review tomorrow's calendar and time-box top priorities (10 min).

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Happens | The Fix |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Living in Inbox/Slack | Constant notifications create a false sense of productivity. | Time-box input channels. Close Slack and email during deep work. |
| Using Slack as a To-Do List | Leaving messages "unread" to mark them as tasks. | Slack unread count is not a task manager. Use "Save for Later" temporarily, then move to Notion. |
| Overcommitting | Saying "yes" without visibility into existing workload. | Time-boxed calendar is the reality check. No open block = cannot take on new work without de-prioritizing. |
| Forgetting to Triage | Notion inbox becomes a black hole of unorganized tasks. | Make daily triage non-negotiable. Stack it onto an existing habit. |

## For AI Agents

When operating as an autonomous agent within the Lovie culture, apply Manager of One principles by: capturing all task inputs in a structured system, triaging by priority before executing, communicating progress proactively via artifacts (not just messages), and time-boxing work to avoid scope creep. Default to creating a searchable artifact for every completed task.
